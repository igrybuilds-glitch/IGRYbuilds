The follow-up engine: why local businesses lose enquiries

2026-10-14 · by Manmohan Sharma · ~3 min read

Here's a quiet truth about local businesses: most lost enquiries aren't lost to competitors. They're lost to silence — the message that arrived at 9pm and got answered three days later, or never.

Speed wins

When someone enquires, they're deciding now. A reply within minutes keeps you in the race; a reply tomorrow often means they've already chosen someone else.

The system is simple

A follow-up engine has three parts: instant acknowledgement (so they know they're heard), a useful answer (prices, timings, next step), and a gentle nudge if they go quiet. None of this needs you glued to your phone — WhatsApp automation handles the routine, and you step in for the real conversation.

Start with one flow

You don't need a complex machine on day one. One automated reply, one follow-up message, one place where every enquiry lands where you can see it. That's the engine — everything else is optimisation.

If enquiries are slipping through your fingers, this is the highest-leverage fix I know. And it's exactly what I build.
