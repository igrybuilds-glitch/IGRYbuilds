How a project runs

From first call to launch, one clear path.

A website project here is deliberately simple. You deal with the founder directly, you always know what happens next, what it costs, and when it lands. No account managers, no jargon, no surprises.

Step one

The honest consultation

Twenty minutes, free. You describe your business and what you want the site to do, and I tell you straight whether a new website is the right move — and give you a fixed quote. The price we agree is the price you pay.

Step two

Design before code

Every page is laid out from a blank canvas — structure, typography, and the exact words that move a visitor toward an enquiry — before a single line of code is written. You see the design first, and we refine it together.

Step three

Built by hand

No templates, no page builders. The site is hand-coded, mobile-first, and built to load fast on ordinary connections. Enquiry forms, call buttons, and WhatsApp are wired in, so enquiries reach you directly.

Step four

Launch and handover

You own everything — the code, the design, the domain in your name. Before launch we walk through the whole site together in plain language, so changing a photo or a line of text never feels scary.

Straight answers

Questions, answered.

How much will a website cost me?

A fixed quote, agreed on a free 20-minute consultation — the price we agree is the price you pay. The three plans are priced in the open on the pricing page.

How will new customers actually reach me through the site?

Through enquiry forms, direct call buttons, and WhatsApp — all wired into the site, so enquiries land with you instead of sitting in an inbox you never check.

Will the site look good and load fast on phones?

Yes — every site is designed mobile-first and built to load fast on ordinary mobile connections, because that is where most of your customers will find you.

Can the site grow with my business later?

Yes — it is easy to extend as you grow, and basic on-page SEO is built in from day one, so you are not starting from zero when you add pages or services.
