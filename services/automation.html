Straight answers

Questions, answered.

Which parts of my business can actually be automated?

The repetitive ones: enquiry capture and instant follow-up, payment reminders and invoice flows, digitising registers and spreadsheets, and WhatsApp and email busywork. The consultation identifies your biggest time leaks first.

Do I need to buy new software or subscriptions?

Not necessarily. Automations are built on reliable, proven tools — you approve the setup on the consultation, and everything is documented so you own it, with nothing locked away.

What happens if an automation breaks?

Every automation is documented for you in plain terms, so nothing is a black box. If something stops working, you can see exactly where — and I am a message away to fix it.

Will automation make my business feel robotic to customers?

No — it handles the repetitive follow-ups and reminders, so your people spend their time on real conversations instead of busywork. Customers just notice faster replies.
