The founder

I'm Manmohan Sharma.

I'm 21, from Ratangarh in Rajasthan's Shekhawati region, and I started IGRYbuilds in March 2026 with one belief: a small business in Rajasthan deserves the same digital firepower as a big brand in Mumbai.

I'm a BSc Maths student. Maths taught me to break hard problems into small, solvable steps — and that's exactly how I build. Every website and automation is engineered, tested and finished by hand, one business at a time.

"So I build it — premium websites and smart automation, by hand, one business at a time."

IGRYbuilds is deliberately small and founder-led. When you work with us, you talk to me — not a sales team, not a ticket queue. I design, I build, and I answer your messages.
