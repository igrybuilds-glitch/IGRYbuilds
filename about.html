The founder

I'm Manmohan Sharma

21, from Ratangarh, Rajasthan.

I founded IGRYbuilds in March 2026 on one belief: a small business here deserves the same digital firepower as a big brand anywhere.

I'm a BSc Maths student. Maths taught me to break hard problems into small, solvable steps — and that's exactly how I build. Every website and automation is engineered, not decorated.

IGRYbuilds is deliberately small and founder-led. When you work with us, you talk to me — not a sales team, not a ticket queue. I design, I build, and I answer.
